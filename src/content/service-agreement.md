# Jacob Websites --- Website Services & Care Agreement

**Last updated: October 2026**

These terms apply when incorporated into an accepted proposal, statement
of work, or other service agreement with Jacob Websites.

This Website Services & Care Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into
between **Jacob Ryan Wainwright, doing business as Jacob Websites
("Provider")**, and the client identified in the applicable proposal,
order, invoice, statement of work, or signature section ("Client").

This Agreement describes the terms under which Provider may design,
develop, host, maintain, or otherwise provide website services to
Client.

## 1. Services

Provider will provide the website design, development, hosting,
maintenance, or related services described in the Client's accepted
proposal, service selection, or written scope of work ("Services").

Website project services and monthly care plan services are separate
services. Client may purchase a website project without purchasing a
monthly care plan.

Only Services expressly included in the agreed scope are included.

Requests outside that scope may require a separate quote and Client
approval.

## 2. Website Project

If Provider is designing or developing a website for Client, the agreed
project scope will identify the pages, functionality, deliverables, and
other material requirements included in the project.

Work or functionality not included in that scope may be treated as
additional work.

Provider may use reasonable professional judgment regarding
implementation, technology, responsive behavior, performance,
accessibility, and design details unless the parties agree otherwise.

Project revisions, acceptance, and Client delays are governed by Section
13.

## 3. Monthly Website Care Services

Monthly care plans are optional and are separate from the website
project fee.

If Client subscribes to a monthly Website Care or Website Growth plan,
Provider will provide the services described in the selected plan for
each paid billing period.

Monthly services may include managed hosting, monitoring, technical
maintenance, routine content changes, performance work, analytics
review, and SEO-related maintenance depending on the selected plan.

Monthly update allowances expire at the end of each billing period and
do not roll over.

Monthly allowances cannot be accumulated or exchanged for cash, credits,
or unrelated services.

Client is not required to purchase or maintain a care plan in order to
purchase a website project.

## 4. Routine Updates

Routine updates generally mean modifications to existing website content
or components that do not materially change the website's architecture
or functionality.

Examples may include:

-   Text edits;
-   Image replacements;
-   Hours or contact-information changes;
-   Updates to existing service descriptions;
-   Testimonial updates;
-   Certification updates; and
-   Minor layout or formatting adjustments.

Provider will determine reasonably and in good faith whether a request
is a routine update or constitutes additional project work.

## 5. Additional Work

Unless expressly included in Client's project scope or selected care
plan, the following may require a separate quote:

-   Major redesigns;
-   New websites;
-   Major new pages;
-   E-commerce functionality;
-   Customer accounts or portals;
-   Booking or appointment systems;
-   Complex calculators;
-   Custom databases;
-   Major integrations;
-   Large content migrations;
-   Major copywriting;
-   Branding or logo work;
-   Photography or video;
-   Paid advertising;
-   Social-media management;
-   Major SEO campaigns; and
-   Other substantial new functionality.

Provider will obtain Client approval before beginning separately
billable work.

For Clients without an active care plan, post-launch services are
governed by Section 23.

## 6. Hosting

If managed hosting is included in Client's care plan, Provider may host
or deploy the website using third-party infrastructure selected by
Provider.

Provider may change infrastructure providers when reasonably necessary
for security, reliability, performance, cost, compatibility, or service
continuity.

Normal hosting usage is included only to the extent stated in Client's
selected plan.

Unusually high traffic, bandwidth, storage, processing, API usage, or
other infrastructure requirements may require additional fees or
migration to another hosting arrangement.

Provider will notify Client before intentionally implementing a material
increase in recurring hosting charges.

If Client does not maintain an active care plan that includes managed
hosting, handoff and responsibility for hosting and ongoing operation
are governed by Section 23.

## 7. Domain Name

Client is the registrant and owner of Client's domain name unless the
parties expressly agree otherwise in writing.

Client is responsible for maintaining the domain registration, account
access, accurate registration information, and payment of all domain
renewal fees.

If Provider assists with DNS or domain configuration, Client authorizes
Provider to make changes reasonably necessary to connect and operate the
website.

Provider is not responsible for downtime, loss, expiration, suspension,
or other problems resulting from Client's failure to maintain, renew,
secure, or retain control of the domain or domain account.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The website may rely on third-party providers for hosting, DNS,
analytics, email delivery, forms, fonts, APIs, payment processing,
booking, or other functionality.

Provider does not control those third parties and cannot guarantee
uninterrupted availability of their services.

Client is responsible for third-party charges unless the applicable
service is expressly included in Client's plan or project scope.

Provider will not knowingly purchase a material new paid third-party
service on Client's behalf without Client's approval.

## 9. Maintenance and Support

Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain
functionality covered by Client's active care plan.

Routine support requests will generally be acknowledged according to the
service level described in Client's selected plan.

Response targets are not guaranteed completion times.

Unless separately agreed in writing, Provider does not provide
guaranteed 24-hour or seven-day emergency support.

Ongoing services for Clients without an active care plan are governed by
Section 23.

## 10. Uptime

Provider will use reasonable efforts to keep Client's website available
and functional when Provider is responsible for managed hosting under an
active care plan.

Client acknowledges that websites and Internet services may experience
downtime due to maintenance, software defects, cyberattacks, third-party
outages, network failures, force majeure events, or other circumstances.

Provider does not guarantee 100% uptime.

Provider's responsibilities after managed hosting ends are governed by
Section 23.

## 11. Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to:

-   Provide accurate content and information;
-   Respond reasonably to requests necessary to complete work;
-   Maintain ownership and payment of Client-controlled accounts;
-   Protect account credentials;
-   Avoid making or authorizing changes that may interfere with
    Provider's work without appropriate coordination;
-   Obtain necessary rights to content supplied to Provider; and
-   Notify Provider promptly of known website problems.

Client represents that it has the right to use materials it supplies to
Provider.

## 12. Client Content

Client is responsible for the legality, accuracy, and rights associated
with content supplied by Client, including text, trademarks,
photographs, videos, testimonials, logos, and other materials.

Provider may refuse to publish material that Provider reasonably
believes is unlawful, infringing, malicious, fraudulent, or otherwise
inappropriate for the service.

## 13. Revisions, Acceptance, and Client Delays

Unless otherwise stated in the applicable proposal or statement of work,
each website project includes **two (2) rounds of revisions**.

A revision round means a consolidated set of reasonable changes
requested by Client in response to a version of the project presented
for review.

Additional revision rounds, substantial changes in direction, or
requests outside the agreed scope may be separately quoted and billed.

When Provider presents the final deliverables to Client for review,
Client will have **five (5) business days** to provide written notice
identifying any material issue or requested revision that falls within
the agreed scope.

If Client does not respond within that period, the final deliverables
will be considered accepted. The final deliverables will also be
considered accepted if Client expressly approves them or once Client
authorizes or permits the website to go live.

Acceptance of the final deliverables causes the remaining project
balance to become due as provided in Section 20.

Client delays may affect the project schedule. If Client fails to
provide reasonably requested feedback, content, approvals, access, or
other required information for **thirty (30) consecutive days**,
Provider may close the project upon written notice.

If a project is closed because of Client inactivity, the project deposit
is non-refundable, any unpaid fees for work already completed are due,
and restarting the project may require a new schedule, revised scope,
and/or new quote.

## 14. Ownership

Upon payment in full of the applicable website project fee and any other
amounts due for the project, Client owns the final client-specific
website deliverables created specifically for Client, except for
Provider Materials and third-party materials.

Provider is not required to launch, transfer, or hand off the final
website deliverables until the applicable project fee and other amounts
due for the project have been paid in full.

"Provider Materials" include Provider's pre-existing or generally
reusable:

-   Tools;
-   Libraries;
-   Components;
-   Utilities;
-   Development methods;
-   Templates;
-   Systems;
-   Know-how; and
-   General-purpose code.

Provider retains ownership of Provider Materials but grants Client the
rights reasonably necessary to operate the completed website as
delivered.

Third-party software and materials remain subject to their respective
licenses.

Client retains ownership of Client's business content, trademarks,
logos, and other materials supplied by Client.

Handoff and ongoing responsibilities for Clients without an active care
plan are governed by Section 23.

permission to identify Client as a customer and display publicly
available portions of the completed website for Provider's portfolio,

Provider will not knowingly disclose Client credentials, private
customer information, confidential analytics, or other non-public
sensitive information for portfolio purposes.

and experience.

Provider may perform technical or on-page search-engine optimization

Search engines and third-party platforms operate independently of

## 20. Fees and Billing

Client agrees to pay the fees identified in the accepted proposal,
invoice, service selection, statement of work, or other written
agreement.

Unless otherwise stated in writing, website project fees are paid as
follows:

-   **Twenty-five percent (25%) deposit before work begins; and**
-   **The remaining seventy-five percent (75%) balance is due upon
    Client's acceptance of the final deliverables under Section 13, and
    in any event before launch, transfer, or handoff.**

Provider is not required to begin work until the required deposit has
been received.

Provider is not required to launch, transfer, or hand off the completed
website until the project balance and any other amounts then due for the
project have been paid in full.

Care plans are optional and are billed monthly in advance.

Unless an invoice states otherwise, invoices are due within **fifteen
(15) calendar days** of the invoice date ("Net 15").

If an invoice becomes past due, Provider may pause work, withhold launch
or transfer, suspend non-essential services, or decline additional work
until the account is brought current, subject to applicable law and any
notice requirements in this Agreement.

No automatic monetary late fee is imposed under this Agreement unless a
separate written agreement or invoice expressly provides for one in
compliance with applicable law.

Client authorizes recurring charges only through the payment method and
authorization process separately presented to Client.

Taxes, where legally required, may be added.

## 21. Failed or Late Payment

If payment for a recurring service fails or an invoice becomes past due,
Provider may notify Client and provide a reasonable opportunity to
correct the payment issue.

If payment remains unpaid, Provider may pause project work or suspend
ongoing maintenance, updates, support, and Provider-paid hosting
services after reasonable notice.

Provider may withhold launch, transfer, or handoff of project
deliverables until amounts due for the project have been paid in full.

Suspension does not transfer ownership of Client's domain or other
Client-owned assets to Provider.

Provider may require outstanding balances to be paid before performing
additional work or migration assistance beyond a standard handoff.

## 22. Cancellation

Unless otherwise stated in writing, recurring Website Care and Website
Growth plans are month-to-month and optional.

Client may cancel future recurring care-plan service by emailing
**help@jacobwebsites.com** before the next billing date.

Cancellation stops future recurring charges but does not ordinarily
create a refund for a service period already begun.

Provider may also discontinue recurring service upon reasonable notice.

Cancellation of a care plan does not affect Client's ownership of final
client-specific website deliverables that Client has already paid for in
full.

## 23. Handoff After Cancellation or Without a Care Plan

If Client does not purchase an active care plan, Provider will provide
the site files and/or access reasonably necessary for a standard handoff
after the project fee and any other amounts due for the project have
been paid in full.

If Client cancels a care plan that includes managed hosting, Provider
will make Client-owned website materials reasonably necessary for a
standard handoff available to Client. Client is responsible for
obtaining replacement hosting before Provider-managed hosting ends.

After handoff or the end of Provider-managed services, Client is
responsible for the website's hosting, deployment, maintenance, updates,
backups, security, third-party services, and ongoing operation unless
otherwise agreed in writing.

Provider may remove Client's website from Provider-managed
infrastructure after the paid service period and a reasonable handoff
period have ended.

Migration, configuration of another provider, extensive documentation,
or other transition work beyond a reasonable standard handoff may be
separately billable.

Provider will not intentionally withhold Client-owned website materials
solely to force Client to continue a recurring plan.

Any maintenance, updates, troubleshooting, modifications, migration
assistance, or other work requested from Provider after handoff or
outside an active care plan may be separately quoted and billed unless
otherwise agreed in writing.

## 24. Refunds

Unless otherwise required by law or expressly agreed in writing, the
project deposit is non-refundable once work begins, and payments for
completed work and service periods already begun are non-refundable.

If Provider materially fails to provide a paid service, the parties will
attempt in good faith to resolve the issue, which may include
correction, service credit, or refund where appropriate.

Provider is not responsible for defects, downtime, data loss, or

Provider may charge separately to diagnose or repair such problems.
