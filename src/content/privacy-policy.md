# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Jacob Websites is the independent web design and development business of Jacob Wainwright. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit jacobwebsites.com or contact me through this website.

## Information you provide

When you send an inquiry, the form collects your name, email address, selected service, message, and website address if you choose to provide one. If you email me directly, I receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or sensitive personal information through the inquiry form.

## How your information is used

I use your information to respond to your inquiry, discuss your website, prepare a requested mockup or proposal, and communicate about services you request. I also use relevant correspondence to maintain business records, resolve issues, and meet legal obligations. Sending an inquiry does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

## Form delivery and hosting

The contact form uses [FormSubmit](https://formsubmit.co/) to send your inquiry to my business email inbox. FormSubmit and the email providers involved process the information needed to deliver and store those messages. You can read [FormSubmit’s privacy policy](https://formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf) for more about its processing.

The website is hosted by Vercel. To deliver and protect the website, Vercel may process technical request information such as IP addresses, browser information, and request logs. See [Vercel’s privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice).

These providers may process information in countries other than your own.

## Website analytics

This website uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand overall website traffic and improve the site. Analytics data can include pages visited, referring websites, visit times, approximate location, and browser, operating system, and device type.

Vercel describes this service as using aggregated statistics without third-party cookies or identifiers that track visitors across different websites. Inquiry messages and email addresses are not sent as custom analytics events. See [Vercel Web Analytics privacy information](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy).

## Browser preferences and tracking

Your theme and motion choices are saved in your browser’s local storage under `jw-theme` and `jw-motion`. These settings let the site remember your preferences when you return. They remain until you change them or clear this site’s browser data. Clearing them does not delete inquiries you have already sent.

The website does not use advertising pixels. FormSubmit’s own privacy policy describes its possible use of cookies. You can manage cookies and local storage through your browser settings. This site does not currently change its behavior in response to browser Do Not Track signals.

## Sharing and retention

I do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Information is shared with providers as needed to operate the website, deliver inquiries, and communicate with you. It may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

Inquiries and correspondence are retained for as long as needed to handle your request, maintain relevant business records, and meet legal obligations. Provider retention practices also apply to information they process. No online transmission or storage method is completely secure.

## Your choices

You can choose to email me instead of using the form, and you do not need to submit an inquiry to browse the website. You can ask about the personal information I hold about you or request access, correction, or deletion by emailing [jacob@jacobwebsites.com](mailto:jacob@jacobwebsites.com).

I may need to verify your identity before handling a request. Some records may need to be kept for legal or business obligations. Any rights available under applicable privacy law remain in place.

## Other websites and updates

Links to GitHub, project demos, and other websites lead to services with their own privacy practices. This policy covers this website and inquiries sent to Jacob Websites, rather than websites operated by other businesses.

If these practices change, this page will be updated with a new “Last updated” date.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email [jacob@jacobwebsites.com](mailto:jacob@jacobwebsites.com).
