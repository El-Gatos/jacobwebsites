Welcome to your sound bed

A loop starts
with a little seed.

  1. Plant something. Each row is a different sound. Click an empty plot to add it, or a plant to remove it.
  2. Press play. The garden travels left to right. Each plant plays when the light reaches it.
  3. Find your groove. Change the tempo and swing, or click a sound name to mute its row.
  4. Keep what grows. Your garden saves on this device. Share a link or download a WAV loop.

Keyboard: Tab to a plot, then Enter or Space to plant. Arrow keys move between plots. Space outside a control starts or pauses playback.
